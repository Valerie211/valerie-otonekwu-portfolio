About Me

Hi, I'm Valerie

I’m a Computer Science graduate using this portfolio to apply my technical background to practical web design and development. Through each project, I’m strengthening my skills in HTML, CSS, Bootstrap, responsive layouts, and visual design while building work that is clear, structured, and user-focused.

My background includes software development, information systems, network security, and technical reporting. I’ve worked on projects ranging from a Django-based web app for tracking tasks and moods to cybersecurity labs involving phishing investigation, IDS rules, and network monitoring.

This site is a space to showcase my skills, track my growth, and continue developing the design and front-end skills that bring digital ideas to life.

Outside of coding, I enjoy learning new skills, improving my design eye, and bringing creativity and discipline into the things I build.
